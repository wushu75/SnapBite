The whole loop

Three taps from plate to log.

The name is the workflow. No forms, no barcode hunting, no database of foods to search.

01 / SNAP

Capture the meal

Shoot a photo, paste an image from your clipboard, or just describe it in words. Whatever's fastest.

02 / READ

Your key reads it

The AI model you chose returns calories, protein, carbs, and fat — with a confidence score. Edit anything before you keep it.

03 / DONE

Save to your day

It lands in your local log and updates your energy ring. Nothing left your device but that one call you asked for.

Why it's different

Built to stay out of your way — and off your data.

Bring your own key

No subscription, no middleman. Paste a key once and SnapBite talks straight to your provider.

Chinese models first

Qwen-VL and DeepSeek are the defaults — fast, capable, and remarkably cheap per photo.

Your data stays local

Every meal lives in IndexedDB on your own machine. No account, no cloud sync, no export you didn't ask for.

No telemetry, ever

Zero analytics and minimal permissions. The only network request is the one you trigger.

Editable estimates

AI guesses aren't gospel. Nudge the calories or macros, fix the title, drop an item — then save.

Featherweight

Vanilla JavaScript, no framework, no build step. It opens instantly and gets out of the way.

One key, your choice

Any provider you like. Even one on your laptop.

SnapBite speaks the OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic protocols, so nearly any model works. Model IDs and endpoints are all editable — when a provider renames something, you fix it in Settings without waiting on a release.
